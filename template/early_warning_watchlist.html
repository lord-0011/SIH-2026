Standard Operating Procedure: OCMS-EW-Rev7

The Early-Warning Watchlist isolates projects exhibiting multi-dimensional hazard trajectory degradation over a rolling 60-day observation window. Conviction strengths correspond to synchronous trigger crossings: (1) Machine-learning risk score acceleration (≥+10 points or top decile delta); (2) Cumulative expenditure-to-physical divergence surge (>10 percentage points); and (3) Physical velocity stalling (<0.4% physical completion per 30-day reporting cycle). Under MoSPI IPMD surveillance guidelines, Level 3 alerts require ministerial nodal officer intervention within 14 calendar days.
